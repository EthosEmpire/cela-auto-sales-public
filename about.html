Our Story

A Family Dealership Built on Trust

Father-and-son owned and operated, Cela Auto Sales was built on trust, hard work, and a passion for cars. We take pride in helping our customers find reliable vehicles at great prices while providing a personalized, no-pressure buying experience.

When you shop with us, you’re working with a local family business that takes the time to listen, answers your questions honestly, and helps you find a vehicle that fits your needs and budget.
